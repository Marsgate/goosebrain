# goosebrain rev 2 parts

Parts for the RP2354 + USB host + internal 3S battery revision. **MPN is the primary ID**, and LCSC is optional, for JLC assembly. Stock was checked on 2026-10-15 through JLCPCB's parts API; the cost-down swaps of 2026-10-16 (build/cost-down-2026-10-16.md, rows 1-21) were checked that day. "DK" means Digi-Key and Mouser carry the manufacturer's line; it was not checked live unless marked ✓.

**Assembly** column: **M** = machine (PCBA), **H** = hand-solder (through-hole), **D** = Digi-Key part, hand-placed (DNP for PCBA: out of the JLC BOM and CPL, pads still on the board).

## Design decisions behind the parts

- **MCU: RP2354A**, the RP2350A with 2 MB of flash stacked in the package. JLC has it in stock, so no external flash chip is needed. The pinout is identical to the RP2350A, so the RP2350A plus a W25Q16JV can replace it if needed.
- **Charger: BQ25798.** It's the active sibling of the BQ25792 (TI's BQ25792 pages now return 404). Pinout and PROG table are identical, and KiCad 10 ships its symbol (`Battery_Management:BQ25798`).
- **Motors and the 5 V buck run straight from the pack**, through the fuse and power switch, not from the charger's SYS output. The charger's internal battery switch is rated 6 A continuous and only 10 A for at most 1 s (BQ25798 datasheet, Recommended Operating Conditions), which motor stalls would exceed. SYS gets its capacitors and no load.
- **Charge current is safe with zero firmware.** The power-on default charge current is 1 A, and no pin can change it, so the **ILIM_HIZ divider caps input current** instead. 100 kΩ from REGN with 33 kΩ to GND puts 1.24 V on the pin, which sets about a 0.30 A input limit. That works out to:
  - about 2.4 W from a 9 V PD source, or roughly 200 mA into the pack (about 0.45C for the 450 mAh pack);
  - about 120 mA from plain 5 V USB.

  Firmware can raise the limit over I2C through the board support library. The pack's BMS is the backstop if it's set wrong.
- **PD request: 9 V**, which nearly every USB-PD charger offers (12 V is optional in the spec). It also keeps the USB-only 5 V regulator cool.
- **3S profile:** PROG = 13.6 kΩ as 2 × 6.8 kΩ in series (R31 + R78; TI's 13.7 kΩ setting, 750 kHz, 2.2 µH inductor, 3S → 12.6 V charge voltage; TI allows ±2 %, and the 1 % pair is 13.46-13.74 kΩ). Read REG0x0A CELL on the first board. TS divider: 5.23 kΩ (REGN–TS) and 30.1 kΩ (TS–GND), sized for a 103AT NTC, with a fixed 10 kΩ (R81) in the NTC's place since 2026-10-17, so TS reads 25 °C (see the pack notes). The divider stays on E96 so a pack NTC can be refitted: the E24 pair 5.1 k / 27 k would move the cold cutoff to about −1.5 °C, below the 0 °C charging limit. /CE is tied to GND. Charger D+/D− are left open; the ILIM pin overrides detection.
- **Charge LED** on the charger's STAT pin (pulled up to REGN), so charging shows with no firmware. Since 2026-10-17 it is a side-view LED on the top edge, between BOOT and USB-C, seen through a 1.5 mm hole in the top wall, so the face of the box has no openings. The GPIO status LED is gone: the WS2812B stick shows status and GPIO20 is spare.
- **MCU power from USB alone:** HT7550 (30 V in) on VBUS → Schottky → OR'd with the buck's 5 V → XC6206P332MR (200 mA, 6 V max, no enable).
- **5 V buck values** (AP64500 datasheet equations, comments in `buck_5v()`): divider 39 k / 7.5 k gives 4.960 V, the same as rev 1's 115 k / 22.1 k. Compensation 15 k + 4.7 nF puts crossover at 14-22 kHz for 30-45 µF effective output capacitance, about 5° more phase margin than rev 1, with a slower settling tail. Scope one VCC load step on the first board.
- **ESD:** SRV05-4 arrays on the USBLC6-2SC6 pinout (two channels per line: 1/6 D+, 3/4 D−, 5 VP, 2 VN). VP is a 5 V working-voltage clamp, so tie it to 3V3 (USB-C) or 5 V (USB-A), never to PD VBUS. On USB-A it sits at 4.96 V, at the working voltage, so leakage may run above the 5 µA spec. The SMBJ20A TVS clamps PD VBUS.
- **LED stick (J12):** an off-board DIYmall 8 × WS2812B 5050 stick (5V/GND/DIN, about 51 × 10 mm) on the case's top edge near the USB ports, on a short cable to a JST-PH 3-pin header. WS2812B needs VIH ≥ 0.7 × VDD (3.5 V at 5 V), so GPIO21 (PIO) drives it through a 74AHCT1G125 (TTL inputs, 5 V supply) and 330 Ω. It runs from VCC, the 5 V buck: up to about 0.5 A at full white against roughly 2.3 A for the whole rail, so **firmware should cap brightness**. A 10 µF bulk cap sits at the connector.
- **Not using:**
  - JST-PH/XH for battery power, which are rated only 2–3 A. The pack plugs into an XT30 instead;
  - reverse-polarity protection, because the XT30 is keyed (the pigtail is soldered once, red to "+").
- **Pack: Ovonic 80C 3S1P 450 mAh XT30, standard size.** Listed 44.1–46 × 24.2–24.7 × 20.3–20.5 mm; the layout allows **46.5 × 25 × 21 mm**. The 50.5 × 25 × 14 mm used before 2026-10-17 came from a mislabeled listing. 16 AWG leads, a **female XT30U-F** main lead (marked on the product photos; the usual RC convention, battery female, device male), JST-XH 4-pin balance lead, no protection board or NTC, so the board protects it. **UN38.3: no test summary is published (ovonicshop.com); request it from Ovonic before shipping packs.** Official page: https://www.ovonicshop.com/products/ovonic-80c-11-1v-3s-450mah-xt30-lipo-battery
  - The **Long Size** variant (Amazon B0D3F6NPMF, listed 62.13 × 16.43 × 19.05 mm, envelope 63 × 19.5 × 17) was evaluated and rejected: its 63 mm length would need about a 105 × 47 board (box about 110 × 52) against 88.5 × 47 for the standard pack.
  - **BQ7791501** standalone primary protector (OV 4.25 V, UV 2.8 V; OTD 70 °C, OTC 50 °C, UTD −20 °C, UTC 0 °C) with low-side back-to-back N-FETs. Path: cell − → shunt → DSG FET → CHG FET → board GND (PACK−).
  - **Shunt: 2 × 5 mΩ 2512 in parallel (2.5 mΩ)**, which sets OCD1 14 A, OCD2 24 A, SCD 48 A and charge OC 8 A. The 15 A fuse stays as the backstop; these thresholds suit the 80C pack (36 A continuous).
  - The balance lead feeds the cell inputs (100 Ω / 1 µF filters) and passive balancing.
  - An on-board 0603 NTC (TH1) by the FETs serves the protector.
  - **No pack NTC for the charger (2026-10-17, the user's call: the packs are used indoors and not stressed enough to need it).** R81, a fixed 10 kΩ in the TS divider, stands in for the NTC at 25 °C: 30.1 k ‖ 10 k = 7.51 k against 5.23 k from REGN puts TS at 58.9 % of REGN, between T3 (44.8 %) and T2 (68.4 %), so the BQ25798 always sees the normal zone and charges at the set current. J2 (the bead's wire pads) is gone.
- **Pack connector: an XT30 pigtail** (16 AWG, short, off-board) soldered to two wire pads (J3, Connector_Wire SolderWire-1.5sqmm, 2.15 mm drill, 6 mm pitch) in the left-end strip, beside the pack's lead end. The mated XT30 pair (about 20 mm long plus leads, 10.2 × 5.2 mm) lies on edge over the strip's shunts/FETs, in a channel drawn on F.Fab.
  - **A vertical board-mounted male XT30UPB-M (Amass, C428721, about $0.26, 70,585 at JLC) was evaluated on 2026-10-17 and not used.** It's the right gender for the pack's female plug.
    - The housing (shroud) is 10.7 mm tall. Source: the Amass datasheet, viewed in the browser, and LCSC's EasyEDA 3D model (body z 0–10.7, 10.2 × 5.2, pins at ±2.5 mm, Ø1.9 holes).
    - The pack's XT30U-F plug is 12.4 mm including its 3.0 mm solder cups (Amass catalogue). Its front 6.0 mm goes into the shroud, so the plug ends 6.4 mm above the shroud top, at 17.1 mm above the board.
    - The 16 AWG bend adds 5–7 mm, giving 22.1–24.1 mm against 23.4 mm under the face.
    - The 1 mm margin holds only with a bend of 5.3 mm or less, so the pigtail stays. The vertical part would fit if a real mated XT30 measures 11.7 mm or less above the male's shroud top, lead bent.
    - The Amass drawing shows the keyed (anti-misplug) end but no +/− marks, so a vertical part's pin-to-net mapping would need checking against the molded marks on a sample.
    - The female XT30UPB-F (C108769) checked first is the wrong gender.
- **Board: 88.5 × 47 mm with 2 mm corner radii**, in a box of about **93.5 × 52 × 30.0 mm** (outline + 5 mm; 30 mm max thickness). **The pack sits on top of the board**, 2.4 mm above it, over the MCU and logic area (board mm x 22.85–69.35, y 22–47), clear of the IO header, BOOT, the CHG LED, edge connectors, the LED-stick zone and the heat sources. Nothing under it dissipates more than about 0.1 W (U10, U9); Q1, F1 and U8 sit in the face area. **IO S1–S6** come out of the left end on one right-angle header beside M5/M6, recessed so the pin tips end 1 mm inside the wall's outer face; **BOOT** is a side-push button on the top edge for a 2.5 mm hex key through a 3.0–3.2 mm pinhole. Thickness: back 2 + flush-clipped tails 1.0 + board 1.6 + gap 2.4 (parts ≤ 1.6, barrier, foam) + pack 21 + face 2 = **30.0 mm**, with no slack beyond the pack envelope's 0.5 mm. If it's tight, use a 0.25 mm fish-paper barrier, or move C5/C8/C12 to 0805 parts. An 85 × 52 box would need ~10% less content (for example external balance charging); see the shared feasibility note.

## Stock and supply risks

1. **IO header J5 (144 at JLC on 2026-10-17).** It is hand-soldered, so buy it from LCSC with the order; above about 140 boards use the 3 × 40 strip (C51953256) cut to 3 × 6.
2. **RP2354 core inductor L3 (4 at JLC on 2026-10-16, down from 9).** Raspberry Pi specifies this exact part. It is now a Digi-Key part, hand-placed after assembly (DNP for PCBA; the pads stay on the board). Don't substitute an unmarked inductor.
3. **AP64500 buck (19 at JLC on 2026-10-16, down from 38).** Kept. JLC covers up to 19 boards; **above 19 boards it must come from Digi-Key** (consign it, or hand-place it like L3). Check stock again before ordering.
4. **Single-source Chinese parts** (CH224A, SHOU HAN USB-C/USB-A/switch, hongjiacheng SRV05-4/SMBJ20A, HL 80N03F): order through LCSC. Each has an alternative listed above except the CH224A. For it, the fallback is 5 V-only charging: 5.1 kΩ Rd on CC1/CC2 and no PD.
5. **Unconfirmed stepping.** Confirm that the RP2354A is A4-stepping at the distributor. As far as found, RP2354 parts launched with the A4 stepping.
6. **Cleared on 2026-10-16:** TH1 (now Murata C13564), the protection FETs (now HL 80N03F) and the charger inductor (now FNR4030S2R2MT). BQ7791501PWR is at about 303.
