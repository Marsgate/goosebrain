# goosebrain rev 2 parts

Parts for the RP2354 + USB host + internal 3S battery revision. **MPN is the primary ID**, and LCSC is optional, for JLC assembly. Stock was checked on 2026-10-15 through JLCPCB's parts API; the cost-down swaps of 2026-10-16 (build/cost-down-2026-10-16.md, rows 1-21) were checked that day. **Every BOM line was rechecked on 2026-10-19** (live JLC API), and the two parts that couldn't cover an order (D4, the sense resistors) were swapped for in-stock equivalents. "DK" means Digi-Key and Mouser carry the manufacturer's line; it was not checked live unless marked ✓.

**Assembly** column: **M** = machine (PCBA), **H** = hand-solder (through-hole), **D** = Digi-Key part, hand-placed (DNP for PCBA: out of the JLC BOM and CPL, pads still on the board).

## Design decisions behind the parts

- **MCU: RP2354A**, the RP2350A with 2 MB of flash stacked in the package. JLC has it in stock, so no external flash chip is needed. The pinout is identical to the RP2350A, so the RP2350A plus a W25Q16JV can replace it if needed.
- **Charger: BQ25798.** It's the active sibling of the BQ25792 (TI's BQ25792 pages now return 404). Pinout and PROG table are identical, and KiCad 10 ships its symbol (`Battery_Management:BQ25798`).
- **Motors and the 5 V buck run straight from the pack**, through the fuse and power switch, not from the charger's SYS output. The charger's internal battery switch is rated 6 A continuous and only 10 A for at most 1 s (BQ25798 datasheet, Recommended Operating Conditions), which motor stalls would exceed. SYS gets its capacitors and no load.
- **VSYS protection (2026-10-18).** The DRV8848s are rated 20 V absolute, against 12.6 V from a full pack.
  - **Soft start:** the old 100 kΩ / 1 kΩ gate network switched Q1 in a few µs, which steps 12.6 V into about 40 µF of ceramics (15–20 µF at 12 V) through the pack leads and rings. C71 (4.7 nF, gate–drain) now stretches the Miller plateau. With R40 27 kΩ and R41 10 kΩ, VSYS ramps in about 70 µs and the inrush is about 3–4 A. A gate–source cap alone would only delay the edge.
  - **C72 (100 nF, gate–source, 21× C71)** keeps a fast VBAT edge from coupling the gate low and turning Q1 on with the switch off.
  - **Turn-off:** 3–4 ms of delay, then about 0.7 ms through the linear region, about 20 mJ in Q1 at 5 A. While on, the gate divider draws 0.34 mA and gives Vgs −9.2 V at 12.6 V (−6.1 V at the 8.4 V UV point).
  - **D4 (SMF14A, SOD-123FL) clamps VSYS.** An OCD or SCD trip opens both protector FETs, and the CHG FET then blocks current back into the pack, so motor current returned in fast decay can only charge the VSYS ceramics. VBR is 15.6–17.2 V; the SMF's dynamic resistance (about 0.7 Ω: 23.2 V at 8.6 A) puts it near 19 V at 3 A, under the DRV8848's 20 V absolute maximum, but near 23 V at the full 8 A six chopping bridges could return. The motor current limits now keep the motors under the OCD1 trip, so that case is unlikely. The first choice, an SMBJ14A (SMB, 19 V at 8 A), has no room on the board outside the pack (parts under the pack must be under 1.6 mm). It sits behind Q1, so it leaks nothing with the switch off.
- **Motor current limit (2026-10-18):** a 0.15 Ω sense resistor per bridge and VREF = 1.32 V (15 kΩ / 10 kΩ from VINT) chop each bridge at 1.33 A (datasheet: I = VREF / (6.6 × RSENSE)).
  - That is in line with the DRV8848's 1 A RMS rating and under its 2 A OCP.
  - Six stalled motors now draw about 8 A, which with the buck stays under the protector's 14 A OCD1 trip (11.2 A at its −20 % tolerance). Before, with xISEN tied to GND, they could reach it.
  - To change the limit, change the VREF divider.
- **Charge current is safe with zero firmware.** The power-on default charge current is 1 A, and no pin can change it, so the **ILIM_HIZ divider caps input current** instead. 100 kΩ from REGN with 33 kΩ to GND puts 1.24 V on the pin, which sets about a 0.30 A input limit. That works out to:
  - about 2.4 W from a 9 V PD source, or roughly 200 mA into the pack (about 0.45C for the 450 mAh pack);
  - about 120 mA from plain 5 V USB.

  Firmware can raise the limit over I2C through the board support library. The pack's BMS is the backstop if it's set wrong.
- **PD request: 9 V**, which nearly every USB-PD charger offers (12 V is optional in the spec). It also keeps the USB-only 5 V regulator cool.
- **3S profile:** PROG = 13.6 kΩ as 2 × 6.8 kΩ in series (R31 + R78; TI's 13.7 kΩ setting, 750 kHz, 2.2 µH inductor, 3S → 12.6 V charge voltage; TI allows ±2 %, and the 1 % pair is 13.46-13.74 kΩ). Read REG0x0A CELL on the first board. TS divider: 5.23 kΩ (REGN–TS) and 30.1 kΩ (TS–GND), sized for a 103AT NTC, with a fixed 10 kΩ (R81) in the NTC's place since 2026-10-17, so TS reads 25 °C (see the pack notes). The divider stays on E96 so a pack NTC can be refitted: the E24 pair 5.1 k / 27 k would move the cold cutoff to about −1.5 °C, below the 0 °C charging limit. /CE is tied to GND. Charger D+/D− are left open; the ILIM pin overrides detection.
- **Charge LED** on the charger's STAT pin (pulled up to REGN), so charging shows with no firmware. Since 2026-10-17 it is a side-view LED on the top edge, between BOOT and USB-C, seen through a 1.5 mm hole in the top wall, so the face of the box has no openings. The GPIO status LED is gone: the WS2812B stick shows status and GPIO20 is spare.
- **MCU power from USB alone:** HT7550 (30 V in) on VBUS → Schottky → OR'd with the buck's 5 V → XC6206P332MR (200 mA, 6 V max, no enable).
- **5 V buck values** (AP64500 datasheet equations, comments in `buck_5v()`): divider 39 k / 7.5 k gives 4.960 V, the same as rev 1's 115 k / 22.1 k. Compensation 15 k + 4.7 nF puts crossover at 14-22 kHz for 30-45 µF effective output capacitance, about 5° more phase margin than rev 1, with a slower settling tail. Scope one VCC load step on the first board.
- **5 V buck inductor (2026-10-18): Coilcraft XAL5030-222MEC**, 2.2 µH, Isat 9.2 A (30 % drop, typ; the metal-composite core saturates softly), Irms 7.2 A at a 20 °C rise, DCR 13.2 mΩ typ, 5.28 × 5.48 × 3.1 mm. It replaces the 4 × 4 mm CR4030-3R6M.
  - The old part saturated at about 3.1 A (2.3 A heating rating), but the AP64500 doesn't limit current until 6.8 A minimum (8 A typical, 9.2 A maximum), and only enters hiccup after 512 cycles at that limit. Any load between about 3 A and 7 A ran it saturated, and the IO header's 5 V pins go out through the case wall to servos.
  - Now an overload or short ends in the buck's own current limit without the inductor collapsing.
  - **Why this part:** the first choice, Sunlord MWSA0603S-3R3MT (7.0 × 6.6 mm), doesn't fit L1's pocket on the board. The pocket is 6.8 × 6.5 mm, between J4, D1, U4 and the pack's edge, and nothing taller than 1.6 mm may sit under the pack.
  - **Ripple at 500 kHz:** about 2.7 A p-p at 12.6 V in (55 % of 5 A, a little over the datasheet's 30–50 % guidance) and 2.0 A at 9 V. The peak at a 5 A load is about 6.4 A, under the 6.8 A minimum limit. 2.2 µH is inside the datasheet's 1–10 µH, and the compensation doesn't depend on L.
  - **Footprint:** `hexy:L_Coilcraft_XAL5030`, Antmicro's footprint from the PartReel registry (Apache-2.0): pads 1.18 × 4.7 mm at ±1.655 mm, courtyard 5.78 × 5.98 mm. It has no 3D model.
  - The "about 2.3 A" rail figure was the old inductor's heating rating, not a limit anything enforced. The README still quotes it.
- **ESD:** SRV05-4 arrays on the USBLC6-2SC6 pinout (two channels per line: 1/6 D+, 3/4 D−, 5 VP, 2 VN). VP is a 5 V working-voltage clamp, so tie it to 3V3 (USB-C) or 5 V (USB-A), never to PD VBUS. On USB-A it sits at 4.96 V, at the working voltage, so leakage may run above the 5 µA spec. The SMBJ20A TVS clamps PD VBUS.
- **LED stick (J12):** an off-board DIYmall 8 × WS2812B 5050 stick (5V/GND/DIN, about 51 × 10 mm) on the case's top edge near the USB ports, on a short cable to a JST-PH 3-pin header. WS2812B needs VIH ≥ 0.7 × VDD (3.5 V at 5 V), so GPIO18 (PIO) drives it through a 74AHCT1G125 (TTL inputs, 5 V supply) and 330 Ω. It runs from VCC, the 5 V buck: up to about 0.5 A at full white, shared with the servos on the IO ports and the dongle (the AP64500 is rated 5 A), so **firmware should cap brightness**. A 10 µF bulk cap sits at the connector.
- **Not using:**
  - JST-PH/XH for battery power, which are rated only 2–3 A. The pack plugs into an XT30 instead;
  - reverse-polarity protection, because the XT30 is keyed (the board-mounted XT30UPB-M takes the pack's plug only one way).
- **Pack: Ovonic 80C 3S1P 450 mAh XT30, standard size.** Listed 44.1–46 × 24.2–24.7 × 20.3–20.5 mm; the layout allows **46.5 × 25 × 21 mm**. The 50.5 × 25 × 14 mm used before 2026-10-17 came from a mislabeled listing. 16 AWG leads, a **female XT30U-F** main lead (marked on the product photos; the usual RC convention, battery female, device male), JST-XH 4-pin balance lead, no protection board or NTC, so the board protects it. **UN38.3: no test summary is published (ovonicshop.com); request it from Ovonic before shipping packs.** Official page: https://www.ovonicshop.com/products/ovonic-80c-11-1v-3s-450mah-xt30-lipo-battery
  - The **Long Size** variant (Amazon B0D3F6NPMF, listed 62.13 × 16.43 × 19.05 mm, envelope 63 × 19.5 × 17) was evaluated and rejected: its 63 mm length would need about a 105 × 47 board (box about 110 × 52) against 88.5 × 47 for the standard pack.
  - **BQ7791501** standalone primary protector (OV 4.25 V, UV 2.8 V; OTD 70 °C, OTC 50 °C, UTD −20 °C, UTC 0 °C) with low-side back-to-back N-FETs. Path: cell − → shunt → DSG FET → CHG FET → board GND (PACK−).
  - **Shunt: 2 × 5 mΩ 2512 in parallel (2.5 mΩ)**, which sets OCD1 14 A, OCD2 24 A, SCD 48 A and charge OC 8 A. The 15 A fuse stays as the backstop; these thresholds suit the 80C pack (36 A continuous).
  - **Recovery after a current trip needs USB (2026-10-18, not yet bench-tested).**
    - The BQ7791501 clears OCD1, OCD2 and SCD on load removal only, with no timer (datasheet Table 6-2), and holds both FETs off meanwhile.
    - LD reads 0.3 × PACK− (R74 470 kΩ against its internal 200 kΩ) and must fall below 1.3 V. Whatever is left on the pack has to draw under about 6.5 µA.
    - The BQ25798 sits on VBAT ahead of the switch and draws 17 µA typical in battery-only mode with its ADC off (about 540 µA with it running). So the power switch probably won't clear a trip, but plugging in USB will.
    - Test this on the first board: trip OCD with the switch off, then try the switch, then USB.
    - The motor current limit above keeps motors from tripping OCD1. The board support library should still use one-shot charger ADC readings, never continuous: continuous drains a parked pack at about 13 mAh a day.
  - The balance lead feeds the cell inputs (100 Ω / 1 µF filters) and passive balancing.
  - An on-board 0603 NTC (TH1) by the FETs serves the protector.
  - **No pack NTC for the charger (2026-10-17, the user's call: the packs are used indoors and not stressed enough to need it).** R81, a fixed 10 kΩ in the TS divider, stands in for the NTC at 25 °C: 30.1 k ‖ 10 k = 7.51 k against 5.23 k from REGN puts TS at 58.9 % of REGN, between T3 (44.8 %) and T2 (68.4 %), so the BQ25798 always sees the normal zone and charges at the set current. J2 (the bead's wire pads) is gone.
- **Pack connector: a vertical male XT30 on the board** (J3, Amass XT30UPB-M, C428721; since 2026-10-17, replacing the pigtail). It sits on top in the left-end strip, beside and up-left of the pack's lead end. The pack's female XT30U-F plugs straight in from above, and the lead bends toward the pack.
  - **Polarity:** pad 1 "−" (BAT_N, the pack's −) at the keyed, chamfered end; pad 2 "+" (PACK+). That matches KiCad's Connector_AMASS footprint marks and the chamfer side of LCSC's 3D model. The Amass drawing shows the key but no +/− marks, so check the molded marks on the first parts. The plug only mates one way.
  - **Height, an open item for the enclosure:**
    - The shroud is 10.7 mm (Amass datasheet, viewed in the browser; LCSC/EasyEDA 3D model, body z 0–10.7, 10.2 × 5.2, pins Ø1.6 at 5.0 mm on Ø1.9 holes).
    - The pack's XT30U-F is 12.4 mm with its 3.0 mm solder cups, and its front 6.0 mm goes into the shroud, so it ends at 17.1 mm.
    - The 16 AWG bend adds 5–7 mm: **22.1–24.1 mm against 23.4 mm under the face** of the 30 mm box.
    - The box stays at 30 mm for now (the user's call). After the first prototype the case may need a local bump of about 1–2 mm over J3, or a slightly thicker box.
  - The female XT30UPB-F (C108769) checked first is the wrong gender.
- **PCB: 6 layers, ENIG** (since 2026-10-18), JLC's standard 1.6 mm stack JLC06161H-3313: F signals, In1 GND, In2 signals, In3 power planes, In4 GND, B signals. Four layers couldn't route around the RP2354A. JLC's 2026-10-18 quotes for the bare 88.5 × 47 mm board: 6-layer $47 for 10 and $49.20 for 30, against 4-layer $14 and about $40 (with 35% duty, +$44.55 per order of 10, +$12.42 per order of 30). Order with the stackup specified (the USB pairs are 0.15 mm / 0.2 mm gap for about 90 Ω on it) and Economic PCBA. See build/cost-down-2026-10-16.md.
- **Board: 88.5 × 47 mm with 2 mm corner radii**, in a box of about **93.5 × 52 × 30.0 mm** (outline + 5 mm; 30 mm max thickness). **The pack sits on top of the board**, 2.4 mm above it, over the MCU and logic area (board mm x 22.85–69.35, y 22–47), clear of the IO header, BOOT, the CHG LED, edge connectors, the LED-stick zone and the heat sources. Nothing under it dissipates more than about 0.1 W (U10, U9); Q1, F1 and U8 sit in the face area. **IO S1–S6** come out of the left end on one right-angle header beside M5/M6, recessed so the pin tips end 1 mm inside the wall's outer face; **BOOT** is a side-push button on the top edge for a 2.5 mm hex key through a 3.0–3.2 mm pinhole. Thickness: back 2 + flush-clipped tails 1.0 + board 1.6 + gap 2.4 (parts ≤ 1.6, barrier, foam) + pack 21 + face 2 = **30.0 mm**, with no slack beyond the pack envelope's 0.5 mm. If it's tight, use a 0.25 mm fish-paper barrier, or move C5/C8/C12 to 0805 parts. An 85 × 52 box would need ~10% less content (for example external balance charging); see the shared feasibility note.

## Stock and supply risks

Rechecked on 2026-10-19 against live JLC stock for every BOM line. Every line now covers at least 144 boards from JLC stock, except L3.

1. **RP2354 core inductor L3 (1 at JLC, 0 at LCSC on 2026-10-19).** Raspberry Pi approves only this Abracon part for the RP235x regulator (its field and polarity affect the regulator), so there is no in-stock equivalent. Buy it from Digi-Key and hand-place it after assembly (DNP for PCBA; the pads stay), or add it to the JLC order through JLC's global sourcing. Don't substitute an unmarked or different inductor.
2. **IO header J5 (144 at JLC).** Covers 144 boards. No other right-angle 3 × 6 2.54 mm header is listed at JLC, and the case is drawn around this one's pin geometry. Above 144 boards use the 3 × 40 strip (C51953256) cut to 3 × 6.
3. **Pack protector U13 (309 at JLC).** Covers 309 boards. The only same-threshold part is the small-reel BQ7791501PWT (C3682023, 31). The other BQ77915xx variants have different protection thresholds, so they are not equivalents.
4. **Single-source Chinese parts** (CH224A, SHOU HAN USB-C/USB-A/switch, hongjiacheng SRV05-4/SMBJ20A, HL 80N03F): order through LCSC. Each has an alternative listed above except the CH224A. For it, the fallback is 5 V-only charging: 5.1 kΩ Rd on CC1/CC2 and no PD.
5. **Unconfirmed stepping.** Confirm that the RP2354A is A4-stepping at the distributor. As far as found, RP2354 parts launched with the A4 stepping.
6. **Swapped on 2026-10-19:** D4 to Littelfuse SMF14A (C207256; MDD's had 4) and the six sense resistors to YAGEO RL1206FR-7W0R15L (C859925; RALEC's covered 66 boards). Both are footprint-compatible; the schematic and board fields are updated.
7. **Cleared earlier:** the AP64500 (19 on 2026-10-16, 3,973 now), TH1 (now Murata C13564), the protection FETs (now HL 80N03F) and the charger inductor (now FNR4030S2R2MT).
